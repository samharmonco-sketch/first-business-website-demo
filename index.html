About the Shop

A neighborhood spot that people drive across the suburbs for.

The Barbershop on S. Cass Ave is the kind of place Westmont is lucky to have. Small, clean, friendly — run by two stylists who genuinely care about the work. Crystal and Janette take their time, listen to what you want, and deliver every single time.

Men's cuts, women's cuts, kids' cuts, fades, buzz cuts, beard trims, straight razor shaves. All at prices that haven't forgotten what a haircut should cost.
