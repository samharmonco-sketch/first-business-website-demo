About Us

A True Neighborhood Barbershop

The Barbershop in Westmont has been cutting hair the right way — no gimmicks, no upcharges, no pretense. Just skilled barbers who listen, clean chairs, and fair prices that haven't forgotten what a haircut should cost.

Crystal and the team have built a loyal following across the western suburbs. Clients drive 45 minutes. Kids grow up here. People who've moved away still come back.
